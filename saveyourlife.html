Your daily goal

Calculated with the Mifflin-St Jeor formula — the same method YAZIO publishes in its help center.

0 kcal / day

How it works: BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor) × your activity factor, then −375 kcal per 0.5 kg of weekly loss (or + for gain), as published by YAZIO. Targets never drop below 1,200 kcal (women) / 1,500 kcal (men), the floors MyFitnessPal uses. Default macro split: 50% carbs / 20% protein / 30% fat. This is general guidance, not medical advice.
